# Technical Debt Community Guidelines

Technical Debt is a space for useful, respectful conversation among people with different backgrounds, roles, and levels of experience.

## Our commitments

Participants agree to:

- Treat questions and differing viewpoints with respect.
- Critique ideas without attacking people.
- Avoid harassment, discrimination, and demeaning behavior.
- Protect private or confidential information shared in the room.
- Disclose relevant affiliations and avoid promotional pitches.
- Make space for others to speak.

## Scope and response

These guidelines apply to Technical Debt sessions, repository activity, and related community spaces. Organizers may redirect, mute, remove, or exclude participants whose behavior makes the space unsafe or consistently undermines its purpose. Until a private organizer contact is published, do not post sensitive conduct reports publicly; use a repository issue only for non-sensitive community questions.
