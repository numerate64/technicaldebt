THE CASE FOR A CONVERSATION

Technology doesn’t happen
in a vacuum.

The pace is relentless. The implications are tangled. And the most useful perspective often comes from a conversation—not another feed, forecast, or conference panel.

Technical Debt creates a regular hour to step back with people who build, secure, support, design, fund, and depend on technology. Bring what you’re noticing. Leave with a clearer point of view.

A SIMPLE, HUMAN FORMAT

One hour. Room to think.

A little framing, a lot of shared perspective. You don’t need to prepare or be an expert.

00—10

What’s on your mind?

Quick introductions and the headlines, questions, or surprises that brought us here.

10—40

Get into the topic

A few useful prompts open the floor. Share experience, challenge assumptions, connect the dots.

40—55

Compare notes

What are we taking away? What still feels uncertain? Where do we disagree?

55—60

Pick up the thread

Vote on what deserves a closer look next week. The conversation keeps evolving.

Cameras optional. Listening counts as participating. Bring a half-formed thought.

HOW WE SHOW UP

Good conversation is a team sport.

01 / OPEN

Stay curious

Ask before assuming. Make space for perspectives different from your own.

02 / HONEST

Ground it in reality

Share what you’ve seen, and be clear about what you’re still figuring out.

03 / GENEROUS

Leave room

Challenge ideas without diminishing people. No pitches; no grandstanding.

04 / TRUSTED

Protect the room

Don’t repeat someone’s personal or confidential story outside the conversation.

GOOD TO KNOW

First time?
Even better.

When and where does it happen?

It’s planned as a weekly, one-hour live Zoom gathering. The regular day, time, and joining link will be posted here when confirmed.

Do I need to work in tech?

No. If technology touches your work or you’re curious about where it’s going, you belong in the conversation.

Do I need to prepare?

Not at all. Bring a question or an example if you have one. Listening is a perfectly good way to start.

Can I suggest a topic?

Yes. Use the topic link and share the question you’d like the group to explore. Suggestions help shape future sessions.

Is Technical Debt about software maintenance?

The name nods to technology’s accumulated trade-offs—but the conversation is about the whole technology landscape, not just code.

Better context comes from talking it through.
